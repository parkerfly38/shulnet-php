Gabbai Dashboard

The Gabbai Dashboard brings together what you need to plan services: the upcoming Torah reading, honor assignments, unpaid aliyah pledges, and members with upcoming anniversaries and B'nai Mitzvah dates.

Accessing the Dashboard

Navigate to Gabbai Dashboard in the admin menu (/admin/gabbai).

Dashboard Panels

Upcoming Parashah

Shows the next Shabbat Torah reading with its aliyot, loaded from the Hebcal calendar service, along with a summary of the portion from Sefaria. If the reading cannot be loaded, check the server's internet connection and try again later.

Assign Honors — Next Shabbos

A quick view of who has been assigned each aliyah for the coming Shabbat, with a link to edit the assignments.

Pledges (Aliyah)

Lists unpaid invoice items whose description includes "aliyah", so you can follow up on outstanding pledges.

Upcoming Anniversaries & B'nai Mitzvah

Members with a wedding anniversary or B'nai Mitzvah anniversary in the next 30 days, drawn from their member records. Click View all to choose a different date range.

Assigning Torah Honors

  1. Open Assign Torah Honors

    Click Edit assignments for this date on the dashboard, or go to /admin/gabbai/honors.

  2. Pick the date

    Choose the service date. ShulNET loads the readings for that date and lists each honor (Aliyah 1, Aliyah 2 and so on, and Maftir).

  3. Assign members

    For each honor, search for and select a member. Leave an honor blank if it is not yet assigned.

  4. Save

    Saving replaces any earlier assignments for that date.

Annual or Triennial Cycle

ShulNET supports both the annual (full kriyah) and triennial Torah reading cycles. An administrator sets the cycle in General Settings with the Torah reading cycle setting: Annual (Full Torah) or Triennial Cycle. The Jewish calendar location setting (Diaspora or Israel) also affects which portion is read. The readings and aliyot shown on the dashboard and honors page follow this setting.

Tip: Anniversary and B'nai Mitzvah lists depend on dates entered in member profiles. Keep the anniversary and B'nai Mitzvah dates up to date in Managing Members.
