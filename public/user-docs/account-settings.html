Account Settings

Personalize ShulNET: choose your language, switch between your roles, change your password, turn on two-factor authentication, and pick a light or dark appearance.

Language

ShulNET is available in three languages:

  • English
  • Español (Spanish)
  • עברית (Hebrew), with the whole interface mirrored right to left

To change language, click the Language menu at the top of any page, or on the sign-in screen, and choose a language. Your choice is saved to your account and used whenever you sign in, on any device.

Hebrew dates are separate: Hebrew calendar dates for yahrzeits and other observances are shown whichever interface language you choose.
Translations in review: The Spanish and Hebrew translations are new. If you spot wording that could be better, let your administrator know.

Switching Roles

If your account has more than one role, for example Admin and Member, you can switch between them without signing out:

  1. Click your name in the corner of the sidebar to open the user menu
  2. Choose the role you want to use

The menu and pages change to match that role. Switching to Member is a quick way to see the Member Portal as your congregants see it. See User Roles for what each role can do.

Profile and Password

Open the user menu and choose Settings.

  • Profile: Update your name and email address
  • Password: Enter your current password and a new one

Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication adds a code from an authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or 1Password) to your sign-in. Administrators and office staff, who can see member and financial data, should turn it on.

  1. Open Two-Factor settings

    Go to Settings → Two-Factor Authentication and click Enable 2FA.

  2. Scan the QR code

    Scan the code with your authenticator app, then enter the six-digit code it shows to confirm.

  3. Save your recovery codes

    Store the recovery codes somewhere safe. Each one can be used once to sign in if you lose your phone.

Appearance

Under Settings → Appearance, choose Light, Dark or System to follow your device's setting.
