Ride Sharing

Ride Sharing helps members who need transportation get to services. Members post a ride request, and fellow members volunteer to drive.

Accessing Ride Sharing

Members open Ride Sharing from the member menu (/member/rides). The page lists all upcoming ride requests, soonest first. Requests disappear from the list once the service time has passed.

Requesting a Ride

  1. Open the request form

    Click Post ride request.

  2. Enter the details

    • Service date and time (must be in the future)
    • Pickup location
    • Number of passengers (1 to 8)
    • Notes (optional), such as mobility needs or a preferred entrance

  3. Post it

    Your request appears in the list marked Your request. When someone volunteers, their name is shown on it.

Volunteering to Drive

  1. Find an open request in the list
  2. Click Volunteer to drive
  3. The request is marked You are driving, and you can now see the requester's email and phone number so you can coordinate pickup
One driver per request: If someone else volunteers first, you will see "This ride request is no longer available." You cannot volunteer for your own request.

For Administrators

  • Administrators can post a ride request on behalf of any member by searching for the member in the request form. This is useful when a member calls the office.
  • Administrators can see contact details for every request, so the office can help match drivers.
Privacy: A requester's contact information is shown only to their driver and to administrators. Other members see the requester's name, time, pickup location and passenger count.
